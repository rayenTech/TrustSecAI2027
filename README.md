🌐 About the Workshop
TrustSecAI 2027 brings together researchers, industry professionals, and cybersecurity practitioners exploring how artificial intelligence can secure digital systems without compromising trust, safety, or human oversight.
Held in conjunction with the 18th International Conference on Ambient Systems, Networks and Technologies (ANT 2027), the workshop focuses on the transition from AI-assisted threat detection to reliable, explainable, and autonomous cyber defense.
🔬 Research Themes
Focus Area	Topics of Interest
![AI Security](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI_Security-2563EB?style=flat-square)	LLM and generative AI security, prompt injection, adversarial machine learning, AI vulnerability assessment
![Autonomous Defense](https://img.shields.io/badge/Autonomous_Defense-0891B2?style=flat-square)	Agentic cybersecurity, autonomous SOCs, AI-driven incident response, secure security orchestration
![Trustworthy AI](https://img.shields.io/badge/Trustworthy_AI-7C3AED?style=flat-square)	Explainability, privacy, robustness, resilience, responsible autonomy, human oversight
![Emerging Applications](https://img.shields.io/badge/Emerging_Applications-0D9488?style=flat-square)	5G/6G security, intelligent infrastructure, benchmarks, reproducible experiments, distributed systems
📝 Call for Papers
We welcome original, unpublished work from academia and industry, including research papers, system and architecture papers, empirical studies, industrial case studies, and position papers.
Paper length: Up to 6 pages. Papers must not be under review elsewhere during the review period.
