🌐 About the Workshop

Artificial intelligence is transforming cybersecurity, from intelligent threat detection and security operations to autonomous cyber defense. However, the increasing integration of AI into cybersecurity introduces critical challenges related to adversarial manipulation, model vulnerabilities, privacy, reliability, explainability, and trust.

The 1st International Workshop on AI Security & Trustworthy Autonomous Cybersecurity (TrustSecAI 2027) provides an international forum for researchers, academics, industry professionals, and cybersecurity practitioners to discuss emerging advances in securing AI systems and developing trustworthy, reliable, and responsible autonomous cybersecurity solutions.

The workshop aims to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and encourage innovative research at the intersection of:

Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity
AI Security and Adversarial Machine Learning
Trustworthy and Explainable AI
Autonomous and Agentic Cyber Defense
AI-Driven Security Operations

🎯 Topics of Interest

The workshop welcomes original contributions addressing, but not limited to, the following topics:

AI Security
Security of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI.
Prompt injection and attacks on AI-enabled systems.
Adversarial machine learning and robust AI.
Vulnerability assessment and security evaluation of AI systems.
Autonomous Cybersecurity
AI-assisted and autonomous Security Operations Centers (SOCs).
AI-driven threat detection, analysis, and incident response.
Autonomous cyber defense and agentic cybersecurity systems.
Secure orchestration and automation of cybersecurity operations.
Trustworthy and Responsible AI
Explainable and interpretable AI for cybersecurity.
Privacy-preserving and trustworthy AI.
Reliability, robustness, and resilience of AI-enabled systems.
Human oversight, risk management, and responsible autonomy.
Emerging Technologies and Applications
AI security for 5G/6G networks and intelligent infrastructures.
Security evaluation, datasets, and benchmarks.
Reproducible experiments and empirical cybersecurity studies.
AI-enabled security for distributed and autonomous systems.
