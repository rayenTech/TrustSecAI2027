

# AI Security and Trustworthy Autonomous Cybersecurity

**Workshop at ANT & EDI 2027**

## About the Workshop

Artificial intelligence is transforming cybersecurity, from threat detection and security operations to increasingly autonomous cyber defense systems. At the same time, AI introduces new security challenges involving adversarial manipulation, model vulnerabilities, privacy, reliability, and trust.

The **AI Security and Trustworthy Autonomous Cybersecurity** workshop provides a forum for researchers and practitioners to discuss advances in securing AI systems and responsibly applying AI to cybersecurity. It welcomes contributions addressing the security, reliability, explainability, and trustworthiness of AI-enabled cybersecurity solutions.

## Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

- Security of large language models (LLMs) and generative AI
- Prompt injection and attacks against AI-enabled systems
- Adversarial machine learning and robust AI
- AI-assisted and autonomous Security Operations Centers (SOCs)
- AI-driven threat detection, analysis, and incident response
- Explainable, trustworthy, and privacy-preserving AI
- Autonomous cyber defense and agentic cybersecurity systems
- AI security for 5G/6G networks and intelligent infrastructures
- Secure orchestration and automation of cybersecurity operations
- Human oversight, risk management, and responsible autonomy
- Security evaluation, datasets, benchmarks, and reproducible experiments

## Call for Papers

We invite original contributions presenting theoretical foundations, experimental results, methodologies, architectures, practical implementations, and emerging perspectives on AI security and trustworthy autonomous cybersecurity.

Submissions may include:

- Original research papers
- Experimental and empirical studies
- System and architecture papers
- Applied research and implementation studies
- Industrial and practical case studies
- Position and vision papers
- Datasets, benchmarks, and reproducible experiments

**Submission length:** Up to 6 pages, subject to the conference and publisher requirements.

The submission platform and workshop-specific deadlines will be announced when the relevant arrangements are confirmed. Please consult the official conference website for updates.
