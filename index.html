About the Workshop

Beyond Detection. Toward Autonomous Defense|

Artificial intelligence is reshaping threat detection, security operations, and autonomous cyber defense. At the same time, it introduces challenges related to adversarial manipulation, model vulnerabilities, privacy, reliability, and human trust.

🛡️

Security of AI Systems

Explore attacks, defenses, robustness, and evaluation methods for LLMs, generative AI, and AI-enabled systems.

🤖

Autonomous Cyber Defense

Discuss agentic cybersecurity, AI-assisted SOCs, automated response, orchestration, and responsible autonomy.

🔍

Trustworthy AI

Advance explainability, privacy preservation, reliability, risk management, and human oversight in security applications.

Topics of Interest

What we welcome

Contributions may address, but are not limited to, the following areas.

Security of LLMs and generative AI
Prompt injection and attacks on AI-enabled systems
Adversarial machine learning and robust AI
AI-assisted and autonomous Security Operations Centers
AI-driven threat detection, analysis, and incident response
Explainable, trustworthy, and privacy-preserving AI
Autonomous cyber defense and agentic cybersecurity systems
AI security for 5G/6G networks and intelligent infrastructures
Secure orchestration and automation of cybersecurity operations
Human oversight, risk management, and responsible autonomy
Security evaluation, datasets, benchmarks, and reproducible experiments
Call for Papers

Share your research and practical experience

We invite original, unpublished contributions on the security, reliability, explainability, privacy, and trustworthiness of AI-enabled cybersecurity systems.

Submission types

  • Original research papers
  • Short papers and work-in-progress papers
  • System, architecture, and implementation papers
  • Experimental and empirical studies
  • Industrial case studies and practical deployments
  • Position and vision papers
  • Datasets, benchmarks, and reproducibility papers

Submission information

Paper length Up to 6 pages
Submission platform To be announced
Submission deadline To be announced
Author notification To be announced
Camera-ready deadline To be announced

Submissions must be original and must not be under review elsewhere during the review period. All papers will be reviewed by the Technical Program Committee for novelty, technical quality, relevance, clarity, and potential impact.
